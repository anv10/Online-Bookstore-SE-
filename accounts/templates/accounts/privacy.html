PRIVACY NOTICE

How we use your data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, phone number, optional name and a hashed password. We use them to sign you in, reset your password and send order confirmations.
  • Delivery details: the shipping address you enter at checkout, used only to deliver your order. It is stored encrypted.
  • Order details: what you bought, prices, coupons and order status, used to fulfil orders, issue invoices and meet accounting rules.
  • Security records: sign-in and account security events with the time and network address, kept for 90 days to protect your account.

What we do not collect

We never store card numbers or security codes. Payments go straight to the payment provider and we keep only its transaction reference. We do not use advertising trackers.

Who sees it

Sellers see the items and delivery address of orders that contain their books. The payment, delivery and messaging providers receive only what they need to do their job.

Your rights

You can correct your details or delete your account from My account. Order and invoice records are kept after deletion where the law requires it. For anything else, contact customer support.
