Common questions

Where is my order?
Open Your orders for the latest status. Sellers update an order when it ships and when it is delivered.
Can I change or cancel an order?
Email us as soon as possible with the order number. Once a seller has shipped an order it can no longer be changed.
How do I review a book?
After an order is delivered, open the book and choose Write a review. One review per buyer per book, editable at any time.
I sell books here.

My account was suspended.
Email us from the address or phone number on the account and include the reason you believe the action was taken in error.
